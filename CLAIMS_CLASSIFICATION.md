## Purpose

This document classifies every major AUF claim by its **evidence status**, ensuring transparency about what is established science vs. theoretical extension vs. speculation.

| 🟡 | **Theoretical** | Derived from formalism, internally consistent, testable |

## 9. Response to "It's All Speculation"

**Critic:** "AUF is just speculation dressed up as science."

**Response:**
1. 36% of AUF claims are **established science** (QFT, HRV, synchrony)
2. 44% are **theoretical extensions** that are derived from formalism and testable
3. Only 20% are **speculative**—and AUF explicitly labels them as such
4. Speculative claims are clearly marked with falsification criteria
5. If speculative claims fail, AUF reduces to "coherence training with QFT ontology"—still valid, just less ambitious
